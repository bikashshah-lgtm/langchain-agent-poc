# Returns Policy
Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Electronics have a shorter return window of 10 days.

# Refunds
Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

# Shipping
We ship across India using BlueDart and Delhivery. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs Rs 99 extra.

# Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped. After shipping, the customer must wait for delivery and then raise a return.

# Damaged Items
If an item arrives damaged, the customer must report it within 48 hours with photos. We send a free replacement or a full refund.
